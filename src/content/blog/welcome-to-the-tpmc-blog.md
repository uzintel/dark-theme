The Tokyo Project Management Community (TPMC) connects project management professionals in Tokyo through knowledge sharing, networking, and collaborative learning. This blog is where we will share what comes out of those conversations, so that people who could not join a session can still benefit from it.

## What you will find here

- **Event recaps:** summaries of community sessions and the ideas discussed.
- **Practical guides:** project management approaches, tools, and lessons learned from real projects.
- **Community news:** updates about TPMC activities and opportunities to get involved.

Posts may be written in English or Japanese, matching the community's bilingual participation.

## How to take part

Upcoming sessions and registration are listed on our [Luma events calendar](https://luma.com/pmc). To connect with other members between events, join the [TPMC LinkedIn group](https://www.linkedin.com/groups/9833230/); membership is free and requests are reviewed by the organizing team.

Have a topic you would like to see covered, or an experience you would like to share as a speaker or guest author? [Get in touch](/contact/).
